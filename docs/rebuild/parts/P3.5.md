# P3.5 Accounting

## Audit (code, 2026-10-15; screenshots from CI follow in "Screen")

**Profit is wrong (A-29)**
1. Cancelled orders still count as revenue. Cancelling books a "Sales Reversal", but revenue adds every INCOMING row and the expense side skips reversals, so the reversal is never subtracted.
2. Refunds are counted as an operating expense instead of reducing sales, and the cost of returned goods stays in cost of goods sold.
3. Cost of goods sold counts only DELIVERED orders, while revenue counts every order from the moment it is placed. Pending orders add revenue at no cost.
4. Cost of goods uses today's product cost, not the cost at the time of the sale. Changing a product's cost rewrites past profit.
5. Any manual expense entered as "Inventory/Supplies" is counted again on top of cost of goods.

**Money in and out is incomplete (A-05, A-35)**
6. A store order books the money as received the moment it is placed (`placeOrder`), including cash on delivery that hasn't been paid. Nothing ever confirms a bank transfer (`isVerified` is never set).
7. Supplier payments (`recordPurchasePaymentAction`) and salary payments (`markSalaryPaid`) write nothing to the cash book. The cash balance misses them, and salaries are missing from profit.
8. Marking a receivable or payable "paid" (LedgerInvoice) writes nothing to the cash book either.

**Who can do what (A-13)**
9. The page is admin-only, but the actions accept managers. A manager can add or delete expenses, create receivables/payables and mark them paid, although the screen says only admins can mark paid.
10. Order-made rows are recognised by their description text (`/^Order #/`, `POS Sale`). Refund rows ("Refund RET-…") aren't recognised, so they can be edited or deleted by hand.

**Invoices (A-04 leftover)**
11. Invoices are titled "فاتورة ضريبية" (tax invoice) and print `STORE_VAT_NUMBER` (a placeholder), but the shop charges no tax.

**Screen**
12. Five places use `ar-EG` (Arabic-Indic digits). The rest of the admin uses Western digits.
13. Expense categories ("Rent", "Utilities"…) and the CSV headers are English in Arabic mode.
14. 19 dark-only colours (`text-red-300`, `text-emerald-400` without a light variant).
15. CI screenshots (dashboard, P&L, invoices; desktop and phone):
    - Money uses Arabic-Indic digits ("٠٫٠٠ ج.س"), and negative amounts render broken: "-ج.س ٢٨,٥٠٠-".

## Plan

- **One money rule, `src/lib/ledger.js` (new, tested):**
  - **Profit:** sales = orders not cancelled (as on the dashboard), minus approved refunds. Cost of goods = cost saved on each order line at sale time, minus returned items. Expenses = manual expenses + salaries. Stock purchases and supplier payments are not expenses, because their cost enters through cost of goods when the items sell.
  - **Cash book:** money actually received or paid. POS sales when made. Store orders when delivered (any payment method). Refunds, supplier payments, salaries, and receivables/payables when marked paid.
- **Migration:** `OrderItem.unitCost` (nullable), saved at checkout and POS. Older lines fall back to the product's current cost.
- **`Transaction.source` + `sourceId` (migration):** order, POS, refund, supplier, salary, debt or manual. Only manual rows can be edited or deleted. Old rows are recognised by their current description patterns.
- **Store orders:** no cash row at checkout. The cash row is booked when the order becomes DELIVERED. Cancelling reverses only what was actually booked, which handles older orders booked at checkout.
- **POS pay-later:** customer name and phone required. The order is delivered, no cash is booked, and a receivable (LedgerInvoice, linked to the order) is created. "Mark paid" books the cash.
- **Admin-only** accounting actions.
- **Invoices:** title "فاتورة" / "Invoice"; tax number line only when one is set in settings.
- **Screen:** Western digits, Arabic labels, light-safe colours, plus fixes from the screenshots.
