# P3.8 Engagement admin (coupons, reviews, contacts, notifications)

## Audit (code + CI screenshots, 2026-10-16)

**Notifications (cost)**
1. Every signed-in person (staff **and customers**, on any store page) keeps a live stream open (`/api/notifications/stream`) that runs 2 database queries every 5 s and reconnects every time Vercel ends the function. The bell also polls every 30 s. One open tab is ~24 queries a minute around the clock: the Neon database never sleeps and functions stay busy. The 30 s poll alone already keeps the bell current.
2. Staff broadcasts (new order, review, message, low stock) also go to deactivated staff.
3. Notification dates use the browser's locale (Eastern digits in Arabic browsers).

**Contacts**
4. A reply is marked "replied" even when the email wasn't sent. Email (SMTP) isn't set up on Vercel, so today **no reply reaches the customer** while the screen says it did.
5. The reply text goes into the email HTML unescaped (`<`, `&`, links typed by staff are interpreted).
6. Dates in browser locale; `window.confirm` for delete; dark-only red.

**Reviews**
7. The screen loads only the newest 20 reviews; there's no paging and the search box filters just those 20.
8. Status shows raw `APPROVED/REJECTED/PENDING`; "Guest", "Yes" in English; colours are `-300` (dark-only, hard to read on light).
9. Delete has no confirmation; failed actions are ignored (the row changes anyway).
10. Search doesn't match Arabic product names.

**Coupons**
11. Expiry is typed as local time but read by the server in UTC, so a coupon set to end 23:59 ends at 01:59 the next day (Khartoum). Shown with `ar-EG` digits.
12. No way to see whether a coupon is used: no "times used" count.
13. `window.confirm` for delete.

## Plan

- **Notifications:** remove the live stream; the bell polls every 30 s while the tab is visible (staff) and every 2 min for customers, plus on focus and when opened. Broadcasts go to active staff only. Western digits.
- **Contacts:** escape the reply; save it either way, but mark "replied" only when the email went out, and tell the staff member "saved, email not sent (email sending isn't set up)". Western digits, in-app confirm, readable colours.
- **Reviews:** server paging (20 a page) with search/status filters on the server, including Arabic product names and review text; Arabic labels; light-mode colours; in-app confirm for delete; show errors and only update rows that succeeded; refresh the product page after a change.
- **Coupons:** expiry read and shown on the shop clock (`shop-time`); "times used" column (orders that aren't cancelled); in-app confirm; Western digits.
