# P3.7 Employees & HR

## Audit (code + CI screenshots of overview, list, attendance, salaries, leaves, desktop + phone, 2026-10-15; the CI seed has 4 staff and no HR records)

**Time (the shop is in Khartoum, UTC+2; Vercel runs in UTC)**
1. Attendance clock times are read and written in the server's time zone. On Vercel a check-in typed as 08:00 is saved as 08:00 UTC and the browser shows 10:00. "Today", "this week" and month ranges on the HR screens also start at UTC midnight, 2 hours late.

**Salaries**
2. Saving a salary for a month that is already **paid** overwrites its amounts. The cash book keeps the old payment, so payroll and accounting disagree.
3. Deductions can exceed base + bonuses, which gives a negative net salary (and a negative cash-book payment when paid).
4. Each salary is typed by hand, one employee at a time, although every employee has a monthly salary on file. There is no "prepare this month for everyone".

**Leave**
5. A leave can end before it starts (counted as 1 day).
6. An approved leave can be reviewed again and flipped to rejected (or back); review isn't limited to pending ones and any `action` other than "approve" means reject.
7. Overlapping leaves for the same person can both be approved.
8. `getEmployeeLeaveBalance` (21/15/3 day allowances) is never used.

**Employees**
9. New staff passwords need only 6 characters; sign-up and reset need the strong rule (`isStrongPassword`). Emails aren't normalised.
10. Role isn't checked on the server (any string is saved). An admin can demote or deactivate the **last** admin, locking everyone out of admin pages; an admin can also demote themself.
11. When an admin sets a new password for an employee, that employee's open sessions stay signed in (`passwordChangedAt` not set).
12. `updateEmployee` accepts any user id, including customers.
13. "Delete" deactivates, but deactivated staff disappear: no way to see or reactivate them, and their email can't be reused for a new account.
14. `bulkMarkAttendance` is unused (quick-fill replaced it).

**Screen**
15. `ar-EG` digits and dates (e.g. "آخر ٣٠ يوماً"), English times from the browser locale, salary in the list without a locale.
16. 4 `window.confirm` pop-ups (English browser dialog in Arabic).
17. Some colours are dark-only (`text-red-400` on light backgrounds in salaries/reports).
18. On phone the 7 tabs are cut off at the edge.
19. Overview card "بدون سجل حضور اليوم (مطلوب)" reads awkwardly.

## Plan

- **Shop time:** one helper `src/lib/shop-time.js` (Africa/Khartoum): calendar day → UTC bounds, "HH:MM on day" → instant, instant → "HH:MM". Attendance actions, HR KPIs and month ranges use it; the screen shows times in shop time.
- **Salaries:** a paid month can't be changed; net must be ≥ 0; only active staff. New **"prepare month"**: creates pending salaries for every active employee with a salary on file (skips ones that exist). The add form prefills the employee's salary.
- **Leave:** end ≥ start; only pending leaves can be reviewed (claimed atomically); approval refused if it overlaps another approved leave; day count in shop time. Remove the unused balance helper.
- **Employees:** zod schema (role ∈ ADMIN/MANAGER/CASHIER, normalised email, strong password); never leave the shop without an active admin, no self-demotion; admin-set password ends that employee's sessions; staff-only targets; show deactivated staff (filter) with **reactivate**. Remove `bulkMarkAttendance`.
- **Screen:** Western digits and dates via `account-format`, in-app confirm dialog, readable colours, tabs wrap on phone, clearer card label.

## Decisions (owner: "go ahead with full permissions", 2026-10-15)

- Shop time zone is Khartoum (UTC+2).
- "Prepare month" uses each employee's salary on file; bonuses/deductions are edited per row before paying.
- Leave allowances aren't enforced (no rule given); the unused helper goes.
