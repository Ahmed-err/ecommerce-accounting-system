# P1.3 Product media — audit and build (2026-10-05)

Source: all 217 photos of the 136 active products (originals downloaded and measured; no Cloudinary transformations created).

## Findings

| # | Sev | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| M-01 | High | 17 active products have no photo (mostly fridges and freezers); the store shows a 📦 emoji. |
| M-02 | High | Every product photo goes through Vercel's image optimizer (uses the Vercel image quota) although the photos are on Cloudinary, which can resize and convert them itself. |
| M-03 | Medium | Mixed shapes: 118 square, 61 landscape, 38 portrait. 58 photos (32 main photos) have a coloured or busy background; tiles were grey, so white-background photos looked like white boxes on grey. |
| M-04 | Medium | 60 photos are under 600 px on the short side (10 under 400 px), so they look soft on phones with dense screens. |
| M-05 | Low | Thumbnails on the product page used `object-cover` and cut off parts of the product. |

What's fine: all photos are on Cloudinary, none over 1 MB (9.4 MB total), uploads are already limited to 1200 px with automatic quality.
Products of the same series sharing one photo (e.g. Ferre ovens, Unionaire freezers) is expected and left alone.

## Built (code only; no data changes)

- `src/lib/cloudinary-loader.js`: Cloudinary does the resizing with `f_auto,q_auto` (WebP/AVIF), widths snapped to 160–1200 px to keep derived images and credits low. Vercel's optimizer is no longer used for product photos.
- `src/components/media/ProductImage.js`: product photo on a white tile, never cropped, and a quiet "Photo coming soon" placeholder with the brand mark for missing or broken photos. Used by the product card, quick view, product page (main and thumbnails), compare, cart, checkout and search.
- Admin product form shows the photo guideline: square, ≥ 1000 px, plain white background.
