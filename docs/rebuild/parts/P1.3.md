# P1.3 Product media — audit and build (2026-10-05)

Source: all 217 photos of the 136 active products (originals downloaded and measured; no Cloudinary transformations created).

## Findings

| # | Sev | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| M-01 | High | 17 active products have no photo (mostly fridges and freezers); the store shows a 📦 emoji. |
| M-02 | High | Every product photo goes through Vercel's image optimizer (uses the Vercel image quota) although the photos are on Cloudinary, which can resize and convert them itself. |
| M-03 | Medium | Mixed shapes: 118 square, 61 landscape, 38 portrait. 58 photos (32 main photos) have a coloured or busy background; tiles were grey, so white-background photos looked like white boxes on grey. |
| M-04 | Medium | 60 photos are under 600 px on the short side (10 under 400 px), so they look soft on phones with dense screens. |
| M-05 | Low | Thumbnails on the product page used `object-cover` and cut off parts of the product. |

What's fine: all photos are on Cloudinary, none over 1 MB (9.4 MB total), uploads are already limited to 1200 px with automatic quality.
Products of the same series sharing one photo (e.g. Ferre ovens, Unionaire freezers) is expected and left alone.

## Built (code only; no data changes)

- `src/lib/cloudinary-loader.js`: Cloudinary does the resizing with `f_auto,q_auto` (WebP/AVIF), widths snapped to 160–1200 px to keep derived images and credits low. Vercel's optimizer is no longer used for product photos.
- `src/components/media/ProductImage.js`: product photo on a white tile, never cropped, and a quiet "Photo coming soon" placeholder with the brand mark for missing or broken photos. Used by the product card, quick view, product page (main and thumbnails), compare, cart, checkout and search.
- Admin product form shows the photo guideline: square, ≥ 1000 px, plain white background.

## Automatic photo fixes (user asked Claude to handle the images, 2026-10-05)

Cloudinary AI on the owner's account: `e_upscale` for photos under 600 px, `e_background_removal` for busy backgrounds, then a white square 1200 px. All 51 results were reviewed side by side:
- **45 accepted** → `docs/rebuild/data/p1-3-photo-fixes.json`.
- **6 rejected** (original kept): Aknan regulator (poster text washed out), 3 Ferre ovens (shop photo with several ovens), Unionaire portable AC (illustrated scene), Hafab 4-burner cooker (ad button left in the photo).

`scripts/apply/p1-3-photos.mjs` (owner runs with Cloudinary keys + DATABASE_URL): uploads each accepted photo once under `products/p1-3-<sku>`, swaps the URL in the product, skips products whose photo changed since the review, keeps the originals in Cloudinary, logs old URLs. Preview path tested on the local DB; the upload step needs the real keys.

Not fixable without new photos: 17 products have no photo at all (mostly local-brand fridges and freezers, and LG "Inverter"/"Smart" without a model number), so there is no exact official photo to use; a wrong-model photo would mislead buyers.

Follow-up (user asked to find or generate photos for every product):
- Web search found no official photos for BestCool, StarTech, M.T, Haftec or Jabr products, Midea's published range has no 12 cu ft chest freezer, and the two LG fridges have no model number. A different model's photo would mislead buyers, and AI-generated pictures of real branded products were ruled out for the same reason.
- Crop-then-clean was tried for the Hafab cooker (drop ad text/button) and the Unionaire AC: Cloudinary answers "Resource not found" for any new transformation of both originals while the originals still load, so their files are likely gone from storage and only cached. Both may break when the cache expires; re-upload them.
