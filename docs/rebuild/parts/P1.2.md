# P1.2 Product content — audit (2026-10-05)

Source: live snapshot `export/catalog-live-2026-10-04.json` (137 products, public data only).

## Findings

| # | Sev | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| P-01 | High | No product has `specs` or `highlights`, so product pages have no spec table and compare has nothing to compare. Key facts sit only inside names (cu ft, L, kg, BTU, inch, burners, doors). |
| P-02 | High | English descriptions are generic and sometimes drop facts the Arabic one states (e.g. Digi 6-burner: Arabic says 2 electric + 4 gas; English doesn't). 12 English descriptions are under 60 characters. |
| P-03 | Medium | Size units written 9 ways: `10 Cu.ft`, `10 ft`, `10 FT`, `12FT`, `500 watt`, `1500 V`… Word order varies ("100L Electric Oven double door - star" vs "Akai 35L Electric Oven"); casing varies ("double door", "star"). |
| P-04 | Medium | Brand spelling: "Unioneaire"/Unionaire, "Media" (Midea), "Silver Crest"/SilverCrest, "disri"; Arabic names mix Latin brands (ثلاجة LG) with transliterated ones (يونيون إير، ال جي، نيكاي). |
| P-05 | Medium | No brand field: the product's structured data (Google) names the shop as the brand, and shoppers can't filter by brand. |
| P-06 | Low | Possible duplicates: two "StarTech 10 Cu.ft Double Door Refrigerator" (one with a photo, one without); two "BestCool Refrigerator 12 FT" that differ only in the Arabic (تجميد / بخارية), so the English name hides the difference. |
| P-07 | Low | Fake-brand label: the scale reads "TOSHIBAH", listed as "Toshiba". Batteries/regulators say "V" where VA (or another unit) is likely meant. 6 names have double spaces. |

What's fine: every product has Arabic and English names and descriptions, no description is copied across products, all prices and units are set.

## Proposal

1. **Naming convention.**
   - English: `Brand Type Key-spec Variant`, e.g. "LG Double-Door Refrigerator 14 cu ft", "Fresh Twin-Tub Washing Machine 10 kg", "Samsung LED TV 55 in".
   - Arabic: `النوع العلامة المواصفة`, e.g. "ثلاجة LG بابين 14 قدم".
   - Units: cu ft / قدم, L / لتر, kg / كجم, BTU / وحدة, in / بوصة, W / واط.
   - Official brand spelling.
2. **Specs from facts already in the name or Arabic description** (capacity, doors, burners and fuel, BTU and AC type, screen size, power). Shown as a table on the product page. Nothing invented. Highlights stay empty until the owner gives real selling points.
3. **English descriptions rewritten as faithful translations of the Arabic ones** (the Arabic is the source of truth); Arabic is only corrected for typos.
4. **Brand field** (small database change) filled from the names. Enables a brand filter (P2.2) and correct Google data.
5. Delivered like P1.1: a review CSV (old → new name AR/EN, brand, specs, new English description) and an idempotent apply script the user runs after a backup.
