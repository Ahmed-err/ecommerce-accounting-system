# P2.4 Cart & checkout

## Audit (live, 2026-10-08, desktop 1366 + phone 390; walked all 3 steps, no order placed)

Server side is sound: `placeOrder` re-prices from the DB, derives shipping from the city, decrements stock atomically, rate-limits, and requires a proof image for bank transfer. The issues are all in the UI.

**Cart (`CartPageClient`)**
1. Phone: order summary comes after "قد يعجبك أيضاً", so it is below the recommendations at the bottom of the page.
2. Sticky bar says "إتمام الشراء" but the summary button says "متابعة إلى الدفع" (one action, two labels).
3. Cart says "الضريبة التقديرية"; checkout says "ضريبة القيمة المضافة". Same line, different names.

**Checkout step 1 (`CheckoutClient`)**
4. Address example is "الرياض، شارع ١٥، منزل ٤٢" (Riyadh), but the store only ships within Sudan.
5. "رقم الهاتف ((اختياري))" shows double brackets.
6. The coupon box appears in both the cart and step 1. The checkout one shows a "SAVE10" placeholder, which looks like a pre-filled code.
7. The card is titled "معلومات الزائر" (visitor info). It should say "بيانات التوصيل" (delivery details).
8. The 📱 emoji in the phone label next to lucide icons looks AI-made.
9. Phone: "الخطوة 1 من 3" appears twice (in the stepper and again under it).
10. Arrows: "التالي" and "السابق" both point right in RTL.

**Steps 2–3**
11. Bank-transfer panel: the WhatsApp number renders as "249910566540+" (bidi). The blue panel and the blue sign-in box are off-brand.
12. The review step reuses the long form labels ("رقم الهاتف (مطلوب للاتصال وتأكيد الطلب)"), and its card is titled "مراجعة المنتجات".
13. The review step lists the items three times: the item cards, a second "ملخص الطلب" box, and a "مراجعة الطلب قبل الإرسال" disclosure.
14. The checkout repeats the cart's editable item cards, with each price shown twice. A compact read-only list in the summary is enough; editing belongs in the cart.

**Confirmation (`OrderConfirmationClient`)**
15. Guests see only an order number. There is no link to "تتبع طلبك" (which works with that number + phone) and no reminder to send the transfer proof by WhatsApp.
16. Buttons use `sm:flex-row-reverse` in RTL (double reversal) and hard-coded amber colours.

## Plan (one PR, `rebuild/p2-4-checkout`)

- Cart: on phone, put the summary before the recommendations; use one label "إتمام الطلب" for the action and one tax label.
- Checkout: Sudan address example; fix the brackets; title the card "بيانات التوصيل"; swap the emoji for an icon; remove the duplicate step line; fix the arrow directions; keep the coupon in the cart only (checkout shows the applied code, with a link back to change it).
- Replace the item cards with a compact read-only list inside the summary. In the review step, show short labels and list the items once.
- Bank panel and sign-in box use brand tokens; render the number with `dir="ltr"`.
- Confirmation: guests get a "تتبع طلبك" link and a bank-transfer proof note. Fix the RTL order and use tokens.
- Tests: unit tests for the new pure helpers (summary lines, labels); existing checkout tests keep passing.

## Found while building

17. **Bank transfer:** the receipt upload said "(اختياري)", but the server rejects the order without it. The customer only found out when placing the order.
18. **Delivery price race:** the checkout first rendered the built-in city list and only then loaded the admin zones. A customer who picked a city before the zones loaded was shown one rate (e.g. 1,500) and charged the zone rate (2,000). Reproduced locally.
19. **Cart summary reversed in RTL:** labels sat on the left (double reversal, like P2.3). The cart also placed items on the left and the summary on the right, the opposite of the checkout.
20. The order QR text carried a placeholder VAT number (`310123456700003`).

## Done

- **Owner, 2026-10-08:** no tax on store orders, so `placeOrder` charges items − coupon + delivery. `LEGACY_CHECKOUT_TAX_RATE` remains only to rebuild invoices of older orders. Account order pages show a tax row only when tax > 0. Zero prices stay as they are; the admin will fill them in.
- Fixes 1–20. Delivery options come from `src/lib/shipping.js`. The checkout page passes them in from the server, and `placeOrder` charges from the same list (a city outside the admin zones is rejected). Bank transfer can't move past step 2 without a receipt. Guests get a "تتبع طلبك" link on the confirmation page.
- Tests: `tests/unit/lib/shipping.test.ts` (shown rate = charged rate) and a no-tax total in `tests/unit/actions/checkout.test.ts`.

Not changed: invoice pages still print `STORE_VAT_NUMBER` (placeholder default), left for P3.3/P3.5.
