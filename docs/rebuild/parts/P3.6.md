# P3.6 Suppliers & purchases

## Audit (code + CI screenshots of overview and purchases, desktop + phone, 2026-10-15; the CI seed has 2 suppliers and no purchases)

**Stock and cost**
1. Receiving a purchase (at creation or later) adds stock directly instead of through `changeStock` (A-28 rule). It doesn't notify anyone and doesn't follow the shared path.
2. Receiving doesn't update the product's cost. Since P3.5, profit uses the cost saved on each sale, which comes from the product's cost. If a supplier raises prices, profit keeps using the old cost.
3. "Partial" delivery is only a label: there is no way to receive some quantities now and the rest later.

**Deleting (A-36)**
4. Force-deleting a supplier (admin) deletes all its purchases without reversing received stock or the payments recorded in the cash book. Purchase history is lost.
5. Force-deleting a purchase takes the stock back directly, **deletes** its stock movements (history gone) and leaves its supplier-payment rows in the cash book (carried from P3.5).

**Money**
6. A purchase that is part-paid and past its due date shows as "partial", never "overdue". The overdue count misses it.
7. What the shop owes suppliers (total − paid on each purchase) appears nowhere in accounting. There is no "owed to suppliers" total.

**Screen (`SuppliersModuleClient.js`)**
8. Empty charts draw blank axes with no note; the empty purchases table has no "no purchases yet" row.
9. 15 `toLocaleString()` calls without a locale, so digits follow the browser. 3 dark-only colours. 6 `window.confirm` pop-ups (English browser dialog in Arabic).
10. The product picker lists English names only (`nameAr` not loaded) and stops at 500 products.
11. "PO" (purchase order) appears in English in the Arabic search box and table header.
12. The purchase PDF is English-only (pdfkit has no Arabic font here).
13. On phone the purchases table scrolls sideways.

## Plan

- **Receive through `changeStock`** (reason PURCHASE), creation and later receive alike. On receive, set the product's cost to the purchase's unit cost (owner question 1).
- **Supplier delete:** only when the supplier has no purchases. Otherwise the action is "deactivate" (`isActive: false`). Remove force-delete.
- **Purchase delete:** allowed only while nothing was received or paid. Otherwise **cancel** instead: take back received stock through `changeStock` (reason PURCHASE_CANCEL, history kept), book a "supplier refund" cash row for anything paid, and mark the purchase CANCELLED (new enum value, migration).
- **Overdue:** any unpaid balance past its due date counts as overdue.
- **Accounting:** show an "owed to suppliers" total on the accounting dashboard, from purchases (total − paid, not cancelled).
- **Screen:** Western digits via the shared formatter, readable colours, empty states, Arabic product names in the picker with search instead of a 500 cap, "أمر شراء" instead of PO, and in-app confirm dialogs instead of `window.confirm`. Partial receiving stays out (owner question 2). PDF: print the purchase from an HTML page (as invoices do) so Arabic works, instead of pdfkit.

## Owner questions

1. When goods arrive at a new price, should the product's cost become the **latest purchase price** (simple, recommended) or an **average** of old stock and new stock (more accurate if prices swing a lot)?
2. Do you receive orders in parts (some items now, the rest later)? If yes, partial receiving is a bigger change; if no, the "partial" label is removed. (Recommended: remove for now.)

## Owner answers (2026-10-15)

1. On receive, the product's cost becomes the latest purchase price.
2. No partial receiving: the "partial" label goes. The enum value stays in the database (older rows read as waiting), and new purchases use only PENDING/RECEIVED/CANCELLED.
