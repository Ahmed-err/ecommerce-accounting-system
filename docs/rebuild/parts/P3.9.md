# P3.9 Settings & backups

## Audit (code, 2026-10-16)

**Dangerous**
1. **"Clear test data"** (System tab): typing `DELETE TEST DATA` deletes every product, category, supplier, purchase, order, invoice, return, review, coupon, banner, FAQ, cash-book row, stock movement, notification and the **whole audit log**, in one click. On the live store this is unrecoverable short of a database restore, and it erases the record of who did it.
2. **Users tab** changes any account's role and active flag (including to ADMIN/CUSTOMER) with none of the P3.7 guards: it can demote or deactivate the last admin and lock everyone out. It duplicates the Employees screen.

**Settings that do nothing** (saved, never read by the store, POS or emails)
3. Maintenance mode; backup schedule (no cron exists); the whole SEO tab (P2.7 builds SEO from code); free shipping (global and per-zone minimum); minimum order; invoice prefix; cash-on-delivery switch; payment-method list; email notification switches and recipients (email isn't set up). The owner can switch these and believe the shop changed.
4. **Currency** select: only POS receipts read it; every other screen shows ج.س, so choosing USD prints USD on receipts only.
5. **VAT** controls: a tax line prints only when an order carries tax, which is always 0 since P2.4/P3.4, so the switch does nothing but suggests tax is charged.

**Shipping zones**
6. Saving deletes all zones and re-creates them outside a transaction; a failure in between leaves checkout with no zones (falls back to the built-in city list at different prices). Costs aren't validated (negative or blank becomes 0).

**Backups**
7. Two backup routes: the screen uses `/api/backup` (no rate limit, not in the audit log); `/api/admin/backup/database` (rate-limited, logged, pg_dump if present) is unused.
8. Backups are manual downloads only. The real safety net is Neon's point-in-time restore; nothing on the screen says so.

## Plan

- Remove "clear test data" (action and button).
- Remove the Users tab; the Employees screen (with its guards) is where roles change. Link to it.
- Remove the controls listed in 3–5 from the screen (and the Notifications, SEO and System tabs); keep the database columns (no migration). The currency select goes (SDG everywhere).
- Shipping zones: validate (name, at least one area, cost ≥ 0) and replace them in one transaction.
- One backup route: `/api/backup` gains the rate limit and audit-log entry; the unused route is deleted. The Backup tab explains that Neon keeps automatic restore points and the download is an extra copy.

## Decisions (owner: "go ahead with full permissions", 2026-10-16)

- Hide settings that do nothing rather than build them now. Free shipping, minimum order and maintenance mode can come back as real features if wanted.
- No tax, as decided in P2.4/P3.4.
